# Third-party materials

This repository interfaces with CANDOR, Qwen, llama.cpp, LS-EEND, Sandglasset, ReDimNet, Moonshine, Nomic Embed, ONNX Runtime, and other research software. Their names identify experimental dependencies; no ownership or relicensing is implied.

Model files and CANDOR source media/transcripts are excluded. Users are responsible for obtaining each dependency from its official source and following its license, access conditions, and citation requirements. The copied implementation files should be reviewed against upstream license requirements before a non-anonymous public release.
